Botnet Attack

Description:
Botnets are networks of compromised devices controlled remotely to perform malicious activities.

Indicators:
- Command-and-control communication
- Unusual outbound traffic
- Repeated beaconing behavior
- Distributed malicious activity

Impact:
- DDoS attacks
- Spam campaigns
- Malware distribution
- Credential theft

Mitigation:
- Endpoint protection
- Network segmentation
- Traffic analysis
- Threat intelligence monitoring
- Device isolation

MITRE ATT&CK:
T1071 - Application Layer Protocol
